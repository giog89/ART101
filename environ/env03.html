You often walk through your city's garden, you truly do walk there everyday, any chance you can possibly get. You know every turn and walk there is in the garden. You're true to this, not new to this. One day while you are on your daily walk, you start to feel reallyyyy sleepyyy. You feel your body slow down, your brain start to shut down, and your eyes now feel extremely droopy. You eventually go to sleep but then wake up from a deep slumber surrounded by insects you've seen around the garden. And then you realize you're the same size as them! There is a ladybug, a caterpillar, and a butterfly. They are asking you what bug you are and you get to decide!

What do you want to be?
